Method

Schema. cholera_admin1 has the column set and dtypes of the JHU OSF release (location_name, TL, TR, sCh, cCh, deaths, epiweek, ...), read from that release at build time, plus country, admin1_pcode (OCHA COD-AB), obs_type (weekly or cumulative), source, source_doc (file and page, or ArcGIS OBJECTID), vintage (issue date of the document), method (parse or api) and current. Values are as reported; disagreements are listed in checks, not corrected.

Nigeria. {{ISSUES}} NCDC cholera situation reports between 2024 W1 and 2025 W9 (the series ended there) were parsed from Table 7 ("Summary table for Weekly & Cumulative number of suspected Cholera Cases") by glyph position, no LLM. Each state row gives a weekly row (TL = Monday of the ISO epi week, TR = TL + 6 days) and a year-to-date row (TL = Monday of ISO week 1, TR = end of the issue week). Blank weekly cells are read as 0. In {{BALANCED}} of {{ISSUES}} issues the state rows sum exactly to the printed national row for weekly and cumulative cases and deaths. Exceptions: {{EXCEPTIONS}}. Cumulative continuity (year-to-date change vs the weekly column, consecutive weekly issues only): {{CONT_BAD}} state-week disagreements in {{CONT_BAD_WEEKS}} of {{CONT_WEEKS}} checked weeks. Median absolute difference between the weekly state sum and WHO's national week: {{NGA_MAD}} cases (median ratio {{NGA_RATIO}}).

Sudan. The layer's weekly state counts (cases only; no deaths in the source), with the layer's own ISO week dates. The layer gives both 2024 wk53 and 2025 wk01 the date 2024-12-30; both rows are kept and flagged. WHO's national series carries the 2024 wk53 count inside its 2024 W52. Median absolute difference from WHO's national week: {{SDN_MAD}} cases. Coverage starts 2024 W30, not W1: the layer begins in July 2024.

South Sudan is not included: the MoH/WHO situation reports carry national totals, map and epi-curve images, and narrative mentions of single counties, but no county or state table.

Joining JHU. The JHU release's last Nigeria admin1 week starts {{JHU_LAST}}, so there is a gap from 2022-08 to 2024-01 that neither source fills. The chart shows JHU's Nigeria admin1 sum for its final year, then this dataset's weekly state sum, with WHO's national series for reference.
